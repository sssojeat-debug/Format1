# VideoExpress Clip Prompt

Use one copy per clip. Replace every bracketed field. Keep character descriptions byte-identical across scenes where possible.

**Scene [##] — [short beat title]**  
**Format:** [9:16 vertical, target 1080 × 1920]  
**Duration:** [choose from currently available app options]  
**Style:** [project style]

Create one connected cinematic shot for this scene. Match the supplied character and location references. The only visible characters are [names and exact screen positions]. Preserve their faces, hair, exact clothing, and shoes. [Character lock descriptions.] The location is [specific place, lighting, time of day].

At the start, [starting pose and prop positions]. [Character] performs one clear action: [action, direction, and stopping point]. Keep [prop] in [owner's hand/location]; it does not duplicate, disappear, change size, or change shape. The background remains stable. Camera: [shot size and one camera movement, or locked camera]. Finish with [specific pose/framing that sets up next scene].

**Dialogue:** [Character] says, “[…]” in [voice/accent] with natural pacing and accurate lip-sync. [Other characters listen with mouths closed unless speaking.]  
**Narration:** [None / exact narration line and voice].  
**Audio:** [specific SFX]; [music cue and mood], kept below speech.

**Text restriction:** No subtitles, captions, title cards, logos, watermarks, UI, labels, or readable text anywhere in the image.

**Avoid:** extra or duplicate people, face or wardrobe changes, extra hands/fingers/limbs, duplicated/morphing props, unexplained new objects, reversed movement, unintended camera cuts, and dialogue from the wrong character.
